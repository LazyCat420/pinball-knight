# Sports motion references and rig mapping

Baseball reference: [USA Baseball hitting stages, rehosted coaching handout](https://cdn1.sportngin.com/attachments/document/6292-2640936/Hitting_-_USA_Baseball.pdf).
The guide describes loading onto the rear leg, a short forward stride, hip-led rotation,
a level contact path, and a balanced finish. Its grip guidance keeps the hands together.

The sword rig adapts these mechanics into a self-toss:

| Animation phase | Rig controls |
| --- | --- |
| Lift and load | Left hand detaches the helmet; both grips meet the hilt; weight shifts back |
| Stride | Lead leg steps while the hands remain loaded |
| Drive | Pelvis rotates first, followed by the chest and shoulders |
| Contact | Both hands pull the sword around a shallow horizontal path |
| Follow-through | Arms extend and wrap across the chest while the rear foot pivots |
| Head flight | Existing catapult arc and 1.35-second flight, beginning after sword contact |

This is a hand-authored adaptation, not captured motion data. The detachable head and
sword substitute for the ball and bat. Two-segment arm solving keeps each gauntlet
on its grip target throughout the swing. The hip and shoulder groups are independent.

Football uses a separate one-handed overarm action: carry behind the throwing shoulder,
step forward, extend the throwing arm, release a spiral, then finish across the body.
